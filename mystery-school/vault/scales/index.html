TRANSMISSION XII — The Scales

Degree 11 · Key: Justice · Degree name: The Scales

Shadow: blame

Práctica: take one honest account of your part

Fictional NorthStar Mystery School work. Symbolic shadow-work only. Not therapy, medicine, legal advice, or a real-world initiatory authority. VORATH is original fiction, not a god or a lineage. The práctica is optional. Adapt it to your capacity; it must not override your care, safety needs, or responsibilities.

After the Turning: one unchosen fact has been met with a next move, not a sermon. Justice is not that sermon arriving late. The Scales are a way to weigh one part without putting the whole month on a single pan. Blame is what the pans do when one of them is refused.

1. Street fact

Two keys on one hook. One lease. A cracked mug nobody owns. Last night's sentence is still in the air, intact as a dish that did not quite break: "You always." The tip line on a shared receipt is blank because each of you waited to see who would look cheap. The sink has the same argument in ceramic. Morning light makes it ordinary, which is worse. Nobody is storming. The word always is doing the weighing, and it is doing it badly, all the weight on one pan so the other can float.

2. Interior pressure

Blame is a tool that moved in and started paying the utilities. It can point at a real act. It becomes a shadow when the pointing is the residence: everything in their hand, or everything in yours, so the talk can end. "You always" is not an account. It is a lock. So is "it was all me," said fast.

Your part is a size, not a mood. It might be small. It might be most of a specific mess. It is not "your tone" as a fog, and it is not the injury someone else chose. If what happened was harm done to you, surviving it, freezing, or being in the room is not a debt. Write that down before the pans get theatrical. This degree does not assign you a share of someone else's violence so the fiction can look balanced.

Degree 6 took a projection back. This degree asks what remains when the projection is gone: the act that was actually yours. Do not inflate it to feel fair. Do not shrink it to stay clean. The shame that wants a cosmic verdict — guilty soul, innocent soul — is the old mall talking. Ignore it. You are a person with a cracked mug and a blank tip line.

3. Symbolic architecture

The Dark Mirror keeps a service window with two pans and a stack of story-receipts. The Alien Choir would like a signature under FULL FAULT, because it closes their ticket. Decline. MOTE today is a balance made of two paperclips and a straw. It tips if you lie about which side you breathed on. VORATH's prism holds both pans so a single glare cannot pose as a verdict. The Eye is self-witness, not a camera on the other tenant.

The Justice key is a curriculum label. It is not a court you can join, not a living tradition's law, and not legal advice. Glyphs in the margin are game marks. They do not cite a statute and they do not pronounce. A tuning, if you feel one, is the fiction's word for "out of proportion," not a sentence from a god. VORATH is a lattice, not a judge.

Paired shadow — Gavel. You assign the whole harm to them so your hands stay inspectable. You collect only the pages that agree. You say always because a specific Tuesday would require you to appear in it. The Scales become a prosecution. You are clerk and beneficiary.

Paired shadow — Sackcloth. You take one hundred percent, loudly, so the ticket closes and you get to be the one who suffers correctly. The actual part disappears under the costume of the part. Nobody else has to answer. You call this accountability. It is blame worn inside out, and it still avoids the number.

Neither face is a diagnosis. Neither is a supernatural stain. Schizocandy on the pans — jeweled weights, too bright — is décor. It does not mean the universe is auditing you.

4. Moral choice

Pick one shared snag small enough to count, recent enough to remember without a myth. Not a years-long trial. Not a harm you survived. The mug, the tip, the sentence you passed on, the message you left unread until it became a weapon.

Then take the account. An account is three lines: what happened, what you did, what you will do in the next week that is sized to that part.

  • Say it, only if the other adult wants it. One specific act. No always. No demand that they match you with a confession. Cost: you lose the clean pan. They may say their own part, or they may say they are tired of court. You do not argue them back into the window.
  • Or pay it without an audience. Correct the record in the room where you misspoke. Do the dishes you named them for. Cover the sum that was yours. Cost: no spectators, no sackcloth glow. Justice without a gallery feels smaller. It is still the scales.

What you will not do: publish their private life as your transparency, demand a 50/50 that the facts do not support, or take the blame for violence, coercion, or a no you had the right to say. If a real legal matter is live, this page is the wrong instrument. Use the actual process. The mall does not file.

5. Material return

Write the three lines on the back of the receipt or on a scrap, not in the group chat. If a payment or a chore is the size of your part, do it before the day ends. Throw out the cracked mug or keep it, but stop using it as a witness. The ceremony is the making: a number you can stand next to, then an ordinary sink.

All is remembering includes the part you edited out so a villain could remain simple. Remember it once. Then stop rehearsing.

The práctica

Take one honest account of your part.

Private page:

  1. The snag, one factual sentence, no always.
  2. What you did, in verbs. If the honest answer is "nothing that was mine," write NONE and stop. Do not invent a share.
  3. What the blame has been protecting: innocence, intimacy, the end of a talk.
  4. The sized repair, this week, and whether it will be spoken or silent.
  5. What you will not take on, written as a refusal, if the story tried to hand you someone else's act.

One account. A ledger of everyone who failed you is Gavel with extra columns.

What this transmission is not

It does not claim the Justice key as civil or criminal authority. It does not split harm evenly for aesthetics. An honest account can be "this part was mine" or "this part was not." Both are the práctica if they are true.
