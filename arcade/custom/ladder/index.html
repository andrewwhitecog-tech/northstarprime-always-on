How the relay works

Contacts in a branch are AND conditions. Branches join with OR. A HIGH contact conducts for a HIGH input; a LOW contact conducts for a LOW input. Light travels from left to right until a contact blocks it.

This is a fictional Boolean logic puzzle, not machine wiring, PLC programming certification, or industrial safety guidance.

At the controls

Keyboard: Tab / Shift+Tab to move between controls; Space or Enter to activate. 1–3 flip inputs. T tests all combinations. P pauses. Escape closes this panel.

Controller: D-pad moves focus. A activates a button or cycles the focused contact. B goes backward through a contact’s choices. X tests. Start pauses. The left stick follows the D-pad. Touch uses the same on-screen controls.

Quiet display: Sound and motion settings are saved. Reduced-motion preferences are respected. If 3D is unavailable, the circuit remains fully playable.
