1. Verified 2007 Sandtrap & Epitaph Tag Forensics

Every number below was measured directly on authentic Halo 3 tags extracted via H3EK (copper_text_dif.bitmap, copper_text_illum.bitmap, and copper.shader) rather than screenshots or speculation.

Structural Mirroring

Half-Height Mirror Symmetry

Lines 7–12 of the 12-row Sandtrap copper glyph plate duplicate Lines 1–6 at 99.8% Pearson correlation (MSE < 0.46), confirming a 6-row repeating decorative plate rather than 12 distinct sentences.

Information Theory

Monoalphabetic English Ruled Out

Segmenting the texture yielded 366 glyph tokens across 134 distinct clusters with Shannon entropy 6.12 bits and Index of Coincidence 0.0276 — well below English prose (~0.066) and consistent with bespoke Forerunner graphic tiling.

Steganography Audit

Zero Hidden Watermarks

Bit-plane least-significant-bit (LSB) inspection and 2D Fast Fourier Transform (FFT) spectral analysis across copper_text_dif and copper_text_illum found zero embedded ASCII strings, hidden layers, or frequency-domain signatures.

Shader Mechanics

The Real 300-Second Shimmer Loop

copper.shader multiplies copper_text_illum by two scrolling noise overlays on 15s/20s and 15s/25s UV axes. Their least common multiple is 300 seconds (5 minutes), creating the shifting pulse mistaken for Morse code — and sharing Epitaph's salvation\bitmaps\weathering_smear_detail tag path.
