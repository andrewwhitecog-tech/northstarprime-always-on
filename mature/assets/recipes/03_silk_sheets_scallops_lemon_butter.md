# Silk Sheets Scallops with Lemon-Butter Whisper

**Section:** After Dark / Date Night  
**Rating:** R (18+)  
**Yield:** 2 servings (10–12 large sea scallops + sauce)  
**Time:** 10 minutes prep; 10 minutes cook; ~20 minutes total  

**Adult tone note:** Quick, glossy, and a little breathless—seared scallops that arrive dressed in lemon butter. Playful date-night cabaret; consensual adults; not pornographic.

**medical_review_status:** not clinician-reviewed; renal limits require clinician/dietitian

## Ingredients

- 10–12 large dry sea scallops (~1 lb / 450 g), side muscle removed  
- 1/2 tsp (3 g) kosher salt  
- Freshly ground black pepper  
- 1 Tbsp (15 ml) neutral oil  
- 2 Tbsp (28 g) unsalted butter, divided  
- 1 garlic clove, minced  
- 2 Tbsp (30 ml) dry white wine or low-sodium broth  
- 1 Tbsp (15 ml) fresh lemon juice  
- 1 tsp lemon zest  
- 1 Tbsp chopped chives or parsley  

## Steps

1. Pat scallops very dry on paper towels—moisture is the enemy of a good sear. Season with salt and pepper.  
2. Heat oil in a large skillet over medium-high until shimmering. Add scallops in a single layer without crowding.  
3. Sear undisturbed until a deep golden crust forms, ~1½–2 minutes. Flip; cook until just opaque through the center and firm to the touch, ~1–2 minutes more. Scallops should be opaque throughout; use a thermometer if unsure—seafood is done when opaque and flakes/firm as appropriate.  
4. Transfer scallops to a warm plate.  
5. Reduce heat to medium-low. Add 1 Tbsp butter and garlic; cook ~30 seconds until fragrant (do not brown garlic harshly).  
6. Deglaze with wine or broth; simmer 30–60 seconds. Off heat, swirl in remaining butter, lemon juice, and zest.  
7. Spoon sauce over scallops; finish with herbs. Serve immediately while the sheen still whispers.

## Allergen notes

Contains **shellfish** (scallops) and **milk** (butter). Wine is optional; broth may contain **soy**/**wheat**—check labels.

## Safe storage / reheat

- Best eaten fresh. Leftovers: refrigerate within 2 hours; use within 1–2 days for best quality.  
- Reheat gently until hot throughout; leftovers should reach **165°F (74°C)**.
