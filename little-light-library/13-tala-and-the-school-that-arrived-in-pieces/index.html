ROOM 01 TWELVE ROOMS Every morning, the School of Many Rooms sailed down to Moonstep Plaza as one bright building. But on the morning Tala came with her cobalt satchel, twelve rooms arrived separately. Their doors faced clouds, chimneys, and one another - but none faced a floor.

ROOM 02 THE COBALT SATCHEL Tala opened her hexagonal satchel. Inside were no pencils, answers, or instructions. There was only a folded strip of blue cloth. She fastened one end to the plaza and let the other catch the wind. It became the first narrow bridge.
