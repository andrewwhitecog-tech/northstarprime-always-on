Set it up in OBS Studio in 5 steps

  1. Unzip to a permanent folder (such as Documents/StreamPack) so OBS Studio always finds your media paths.
  2. Add your scene loops as a Media Source (scenes/*_loop_720p.webm with Loop checked and Transform → Fit to screen) or as a full-resolution 1920×1080 Image source.
  3. Drop in titles or use your own by placing scenes/text_*.png as an Image source above the loop—or leave the text layer off and add custom channel text.
  4. Frame your camera by placing webcam_frame_16x9.png or webcam_frame_4x3.png above your video capture source and sizing the camera to the transparent window.
  5. Upload alerts & panels to your alert box (3-second transparent alerts/*_alert.webm or still PNG fallback) and your channel’s About page (panels/panel_*.png).
