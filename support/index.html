The Journey & Challenge

Andre is currently facing the fight of his life against End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD)—complete kidney failure. His kidneys can no longer filter toxins, manage electrolytes, or clear excess fluid from his body.

To stay alive, Andre undergoes maintenance hemodialysis 3 to 4 times every single week, hooked up to an artificial dialyzer for four grueling hours per session. Dialysis is life-preserving, but it is not a cure. The only true path back to lasting vitality and health is a kidney transplant.

"Even while navigating extreme fluid restrictions (1,000 mL/day) and dialysis washout, Andre channeled his focus into building RenalShield and open medical nutrition tools to help other kidney patients safely navigate clinical food choices."

This fund exists to bridge the financial gap—covering hemodialysis copays, specialized living donor workup and travel expenses, anti-rejection medications, and post-transplant recovery living costs.

Become a Living Kidney Donor

A living donor kidney lasts almost twice as long as a deceased donor organ and can be scheduled before severe dialysis complications arise. You do not need to be a family member or direct blood match.

1. You Only Need One Kidney

Humans are born with two kidneys but only need one to live a full, normal, athletic life with zero medications.

2. 100% Medical Cost Coverage

All donor screening, medical workup, surgery, hospital stay, and follow-up are fully covered by recipient insurance.

3. National Paired Exchange

If your blood type doesn't directly match Andre, the National Kidney Registry swaps donors so both recipients receive matched kidneys.

4. Confidential & No Obligation

The screening starts with a 15-minute online health questionnaire. You can pause or decline at any point with total medical privacy.
