# NorthStar Chrono Defender — 9 November 2026

[Play free in your browser](https://northstarprime.net/arcade/custom/northstar-chrono-defender/).

- **A VORATH space arena:** gold and spectral relief surround a crystalline eye gate, with restrained parallax as you move. An optional, clearly labeled fiction trail is earned during play and opens separately from your run. [PR239](https://github.com/andrewwhitecog-tech/northstarprime-always-on/pull/239)
- **Clearer enemy identities:** the existing scout and gunship each have neutral, thrust and damage poses—six poses across two enemy classes—with their own materials and silhouettes. [PR242](https://github.com/andrewwhitecog-tech/northstarprime-always-on/pull/242)
- **More room in portrait:** at a 390×844 browser viewport, the measured playfield height increased from 208.1 to 350.6 CSS pixels. Controls remain separate from the arena, and pausing retains the combat frame. [PR244](https://github.com/andrewwhitecog-tech/northstarprime-always-on/pull/244)
- **Input status and fresh starts:** the device caption follows observed input, including touch, rather than relying only on reported device capabilities. A new sortie recenters the ship after resizing and clears inherited movement and held inputs. [PR244](https://github.com/andrewwhitecog-tech/northstarprime-always-on/pull/244)

- **Distinct shots and real hit feedback:** twin plasma, tachyon beam and micro-missiles now use three original flight designs and matching brief impact art. Failed or unavailable artwork retains the existing geometric fallback. Damage, weapon timing and collision rules are unchanged by this artwork update. [PR246](https://github.com/andrewwhitecog-tech/northstarprime-always-on/pull/246)

The [public release check](https://github.com/andrewwhitecog-tech/northstarprime-always-on/actions/runs/42586080818) verified the exact shipped engine, weapon module and four art assets, plus native browser movement and touch aiming/firing across desktop and both phone layouts, with no page errors. Before release, 63 component tests and 60 browser checks covered the weapon art and actual hit effects. These checks do not establish physical-device performance.

Readability inspiration came from the [official Nova Drift presskit](https://novadrift.io/presskit.html) and [Geometry Wars 3: Dimensions Evolved on Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/310790/). Their artwork is not included, and no gameplay-parity claim is made.

Physical phone/controller testing, sustained performance, balance and gameplay depth, audio and VFX improvements, and overall visual and gameplay quality remain open.
