WIND 01 THE KNOTTED SKY The morning after the great storm, every weather ribbon in Bellweather was tangled around the tower. Windmills stood still. Laundry hung like painted flags. Even the clouds seemed to be holding their breath. Sana looked up. "Winds aren't ours to keep," she said.
