    MAKE YOUR OWN QUIET

    Five rituals. One good night.

    1. Raid the fridge.
    2. Choose a record.
    3. Spend 12 seconds on the couch.
    4. Catch three slow breaths by the window.
    5. Clear Ripple or catch 12 snacks in Munch.

    Finish the rituals, then head to the door before midnight. Your night saves automatically. The tray is optional; every ritual is reachable without it.
