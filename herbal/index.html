The Master Herbal Bible:
Art & Botany Edition

The Master Herbal Bible: Art & Botany Edition is a 69-page illustrated book about plants: how they are built, how people have drawn and pressed them for five centuries, and the stories that grew up around them.

Download PDF · about 32 MB

Inside

  • how a plant is put together: leaves, flowers, roots, and what you see under a microscope
  • the craft of the herbarium: pressed plants, botanical drawing and engraving
  • 45 plant portraits, from rosemary and chamomile onward, each with its picture, where it grows and its place in history
  • 66 pictures, every one credited to its maker with its licence (page 68)

What it is not: it is not a remedy book. It gives no recipes, no doses and no medical advice.

Format: PDF, US Letter, 69 pages, about 32 MB. A digital file; nothing is posted to you.
