# Lights-Out Dark Chocolate Lava Cakes for Two

**Section:** After Dark / Date Night  
**Rating:** R (18+)  
**Yield:** 2 individual cakes (about 3–3½ in / 8 cm ramekins)  
**Time:** 15 minutes prep; 12–14 minutes bake; ~30 minutes total  

**Adult tone note:** Cakes that collapse into molten centers on cue—dim the lights, share one spoon if you dare. Cabaret dessert energy; consensual adults; not pornographic.

**medical_review_status:** not clinician-reviewed; renal limits require clinician/dietitian

## Ingredients

- Butter and cocoa powder for ramekins  
- 3 oz (85 g) dark chocolate (60–70%), chopped  
- 3 Tbsp (42 g) unsalted butter  
- 1 large egg + 1 large egg yolk  
- 3 Tbsp (40 g) granulated sugar  
- 1/4 tsp vanilla extract  
- Pinch of salt  
- 2 Tbsp (16 g) all-purpose flour  
- Optional: powdered sugar or berries to finish  

## Steps

1. Heat oven to 425°F (220°C). Butter two ramekins; dust with cocoa; tap out excess. Place on a small baking sheet.  
2. Melt chocolate and butter together in a heatproof bowl over barely simmering water (or short microwave bursts), stirring until smooth. Cool 2–3 minutes.  
3. Whisk egg, yolk, sugar, vanilla, and salt until slightly thick and pale, ~1 minute.  
4. Whisk chocolate mixture into eggs. Fold in flour until just combined—do not overmix.  
5. Divide batter between ramekins. Bake 12–14 minutes until edges are set and centers still soft/jiggly.  
6. Rest 1 minute. Run a knife around edges; invert onto plates (or eat from ramekins). Dust and serve immediately while the center still runs.

## Allergen notes

Contains **milk**, **eggs**, **wheat/gluten**, and often **soy** (in chocolate). Check chocolate labels for **tree nuts** cross-contact if relevant.

## Safe storage / reheat

- Best fresh. Unbaked batter in ramekins can be refrigerated covered up to 4 hours; add 1–2 minutes bake time from cold.  
- Leftover baked cakes: refrigerate within 2 hours; use within 2–3 days. Reheat gently until hot throughout; leftovers to **165°F (74°C)**. Centers will set more after refrigeration.  
- USDA FSIS leftovers: https://www.fsis.usda.gov/food-safety/safe-food-handling-and-preparation/food-safety-basics/leftovers-and-food-safety  
- Safe temperature chart: https://www.fsis.usda.gov/food-safety/safe-food-handling-and-preparation/food-safety-basics/safe-temperature-chart  
- Egg dishes: cook until yolks/whites firm as appropriate for the recipe style; leftover egg-containing dishes reheat to 165°F per USDA FSIS chart.
