REFRACTION Skirmish Game

In the world of VORATH, crystal does not simply absorb wounds—it shatters, and in shattering, it redirects light. An original, lightning-fast d6 tabletop game included free with every pledge.

Download Quickstart PDF (11.6 MB) ✦

1. The Fracture Die (d6)

When hit, roll the Fracture Die: 1–3 causes crystalline stress; 4–5 triggers an Ascension Surge (free reaction attack); 6 detonates the model in a lethal shrapnel burst!

2. Prism Beam Banking

Certain leaders bend line-of-sight around physical obstacles. Laser attacks can literally bank off friendly crystal armor to strike foes behind heavy cover.

3. Fast ~30 Min Matches

Designed for 5–10 models per side with alternating activations. Dynamic, decisive, and packed with high-stakes emergent moments.

✦

Print Protocol: Warm-Water Support Removal

After washing your uncured print in 99% Isopropyl Alcohol (IPA), do not cure immediately! Soak the model in warm water (45°C–50°C / 115°F–120°F) for 45 seconds. The resin supports will soften like warm vinyl, peeling away effortlessly with zero pitting or cratering on fine crystal facets. Allow to fully air-dry before final UV post-cure.
