45-Second Teaser Script

A neighboring broadcast breaks through with eight short stories, each centered on one strange object and one practical cost.

IDC Anthology · Episode 1
Arrival
A NorthStar broadcast slips out of phase and discovers a neighboring dimension that only answers practical questions.
Object: A brass station knob labeled LOCAL ONLY.
Rule: The neighboring reality refuses abstraction and converts every question into an actionable local task.
IDC Anthology · Episode 2
Misread Signal
The station decodes a distress call as a weather report and accidentally turns the city into a machine for solving the wrong problems.
Object: A damp program schedule with tomorrow's mistakes circled in red pencil.
Rule: Warnings become forecasts if the receiver wants comfort more than accuracy.
IDC Anthology · Episode 3
Local Conflict
Two incompatible versions of the same neighborhood tape arrive, and both parties are telling the truth from different property laws.
Object: A zoning map where one alley is labeled MUTUAL HAUNTING.
Rule: A place can be locally true in more than one civic system at the same time.
IDC Anthology · Episode 4
Artifact Cost
A perfect replacement part repairs the station board, but each fix moves an inconvenience out of reality and into the host's life.
Object: A black thermal-paper receipt with no total, only a blinking cursor.
Rule: Nothing is fixed for free; removed friction is reassigned to someone who can notice it.
IDC Anthology · Episode 5
Cross-Dimensional Pressure
A second sponsor buys airtime with ad copy that predicts listener decisions before the listeners make them.
Object: A rate card priced in seconds of attention.
Rule: Attention can be traded before it is spent, but only if the audience accepts the invoice.
IDC Anthology · Episode 6
Season Fracture
Episode six airs in fragments across every previous episode, forcing the host to assemble continuity while the audience hears consequences first.
Object: A broken slate card that says TAKE 0.
Rule: Continuity is not order; it is the cost of believing events belong together.
IDC Anthology · Episode 7
False Repair
The signal finally stabilizes, but the fix removes contradiction, improvisation, memory, and eventually choice.
Object: A perfect transcript of a conversation no one is willing to have.
Rule: A flawless signal is hostile if it cannot carry choice.
IDC Anthology · Episode 8
Final Transmission
A shifting regulator cancels the broadcast unless the crew gives the neighboring reality one honest local story it cannot optimize.
Object: The brass knob from episode one, now warm and unlabeled.
Rule: A neighboring reality cannot optimize a story that stays accountable to one place, one choice, and one witness.
