MOVEMENT II: THE MONOLITHS OF SILENCE

§ 2.1 The Basalt Obelisks

In the obsidian canyons of the fourth celestial quadrant, immense basalt obelisks float unanchored against the gravity of dying dwarf stars. These are not ornamental tombs; they are tuning forks.

When a spacecraft or astral traveller approaches within three kilometers of an obelisk, all mechanical noise drops to absolute silence. The hiss of life-support fans, the hum of ion drives, and the chatter of radio circuits are absorbed entirely by the dense crystalline lattice. Within this acoustic vacuum, the traveller hears only their own blood circulating through the carotid arteries—and then, emerging beneath that rhythm, the deep 24 Hz subterranean drone of the planet below.

"To lift a mountain of granite, one does not fight gravity with violence. One whispers the stone's resonant frequency until its mass forgets how to fall."
