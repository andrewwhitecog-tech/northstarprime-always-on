THE THROUGH-LINE

Pause · check · ask · respond

PauseUnknown potency, mixtures, delayed onset, heat, dehydration, and impairment all change risk.
CheckTesting may reduce uncertainty, but a negative result does not prove identity, purity, dose, or safety.
AskMedication, transplant, kidney, heart, and mental-health concerns belong with a pharmacist or the person’s own care team.
RespondCall 911 for collapse, seizure, breathing trouble, severe overheating, chest pain, or inability to wake.
