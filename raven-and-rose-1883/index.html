1883 William S. Ladd Carriage House · 1331 SW Broadway, Portland, OR · 4-Plate Special Reserve

Raven & Rose & The Rookery Bar (1883)

In May 2007, preservationists lifted the 1883 William S. Ladd Carriage House onto hydraulic dollies and rolled it four blocks down SW Broadway to save it from demolition. From 2012 to 2021, Chef/Partner Dave Shenaut's Raven & Rose and upstairs hayloft apothecary lounge The Rookery Bar filled its Queen Anne rafters with brick-oven Yorkshire pudding, Irish bangers & colcannon, cask ale, and The Bird is the Word.
