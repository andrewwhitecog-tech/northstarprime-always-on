Exact technical deliverables on every Short

9:16 1080×1920 MP4

Each Short runs 30 to 55 seconds in H.264 + AAC MP4 format at full 1080×1920 vertical resolution—ready for YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, and TikTok.

Hook-scored & pause-trimmed

Candidate clips are scored by speech density, vocal energy, opening question/hook strength, and low dead air—then human-checked so every Short starts and ends on a clean speech pause, never mid-word.

Zero drifting pans or zooms

Each shot holds a locked, face-centered vertical crop for the entire shot. No artificial Ken Burns zooms, no wandering auto-tracker pans, and empty slates, logo cards, or faceless screenshares are skipped.

-14 LUFS / -1 dBTP audio

Integrated loudness is normalized to -14 LUFS with true peaks kept below -1 dBTP, so your voice plays evenly alongside major feed content without clipping or volume drops.

Spell-checked .srt sidecar

Every Short includes its own human-spell-checked .srt subtitle file. We keep your video frames clean and do not burn text into the picture unless you explicitly ask for burned-in captions.

3 title & caption hooks

Each delivered Short comes with 3 post-ready title or caption ideas written from the actual spoken moment, plus 1 revision round within 7 days of delivery.

How we cut & verify your Shorts

  1. Send your source video link (up to 60 minutes) along with batch size, speaker setup, any favorite or off-limits timestamps, and whether you prefer clean .srt sidecars or burned-in captions.
  2. Receive a fixed quote & turnaround window before any editing begins.
  3. Hook scoring, locked framing & audio leveling: we isolate the highest-signal 30–55 second moments, lock single or stacked 1080×960 framing per shot, and normalize audio to -14 LUFS / -1 dBTP.
  4. Two-person human review & delivery: captions are spell-checked by hand and a second reviewer watches every Short start-to-finish before delivery.
