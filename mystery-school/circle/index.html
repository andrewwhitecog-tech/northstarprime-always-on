The Open Canon Merit Gate

Andre opened the Mystery School as shared canon for collaborators. Any room, lore, glyph, or transmission is woven in if it satisfies the 7 rules:

1. Artful Fiction

Strictly fictional and symbolic. Zero medical, legal, financial, or occult factual claims.

2. App Integrity

Routes resolve cleanly, images load smoothly, responsive across mobile, zero injection.

3. Design Respect

Hidden-yet-discoverable. Never gates paid core paths behind unsolvable puzzles.

4. Accessible

Motion can be reduced or paused, text is legible, comfort principle holds at all times.

5. Legal Framing

Adult or altered-state themes remain strictly legal, fictional, and responsibly framed.

6. 100% Original IP

Purely original VORATH lore; zero third-party marks, brand violations, or borrowed lineages.

7. Adds Real Value

Deepens the world and returns agency; never just empty word count padding.
