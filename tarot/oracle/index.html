A language of light

Refraction & Fracture

In the VORATH story, creation passes through a prism into Nine Threads. Each card offers an image and a way of looking at experience. Upright meanings are called Refraction; reversed meanings are called Fracture.

The three-card spread asks: What do you carry? What do you notice? What could you explore? These are invitations to reflect, not statements about your future or advice for health, money or other decisions.

This experience is silent. Searches and readings stay in this page; no personal question is collected or saved.
