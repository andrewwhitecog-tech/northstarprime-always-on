How your order works

  1. Send your story brief with the recipient’s first name and pronunciation, occasion and target date, genre/mood/voice preference, and three must-mention memories (plus any topics to avoid).
  2. Confirm scope & quote before production begins; the 48-hour delivery clock starts once your complete brief is confirmed.
  3. Lyric craft, multi-take curation & mastering: our team writes original lyrics, produces and auditions takes for crystal-clear vocal delivery, masters the chosen cut to -14 LUFS, and typesets your Printable Lyric Card PDF.
  4. Second-reviewer check & delivery: a second team member verifies every name and memory against your brief before sending your files, with one lyric revision available within 7 days.

How It Is Made

Honest AI-assisted music disclosure

Original lyrics are written specifically for your story by the NorthStar Prime team. Vocals and instrumentation are generated with commercial-licensed AI music tools, curated across multiple takes, mastered to -14 LUFS, and human-checked end to end so every name and memory is sung clearly. We never imitate real recording artists, singers, or bands, and we never use copyrighted songs or melodies.
