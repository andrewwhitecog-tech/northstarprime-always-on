Brands That Only Exist On-Screen

Prop departments avoid trademark litigation by inventing fictional brands—and then reusing them. Across decades, the same disaster airline, the same smoky cigarette pack, and the same heavy wrench drift between unrelated film sets, quietly stitching a secret shared universe.

The Prop Provenance Invariant: No real airline licenses its trademark to a crashing airliner; no real tobacco conglomerate appears on an assassin's nightstand without legal exposure. Studio prop carts maintain "clearable" fictional trademarks. Over time, recurring props evolve from practical shortcuts into deliberate homage, creator signatures, and canonical bridges.
