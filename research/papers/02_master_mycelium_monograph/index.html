Structured Abstract

Synthesizing 45 mycological chapters and commercial agricultural research from Yamhill County, Oregon (Oregon Tilth Certified Organic OT-011161; ORS 616.706-.726), this monograph examines four interconnected domains of fungal biology and gastronomy: (1) the four-enzyme genetically encodable caffeic acid bioluminescence cycle (HispS, H3H, Luz, CPH; lambda_max ~ 520-530 nm); (2) four elevation-stratified fruiting corridors across the Pacific Northwest Cascadia bioregion; (3) forensic lookalike toxicology incorporating the November 2025 - March 2026 Northern California amatoxin outbreak (CDC MMWR 75(20):258-263, 2026; doi:10.15585/mmwr.mm7520a2); and (4) the two-stage dry-evaporation and Maillard searing protocol for cross-linked chitin/beta-glucan cell walls at 425°F (218°C), a hypothesized, not yet measured, retention model for the sulfur-histidine antioxidant L-ergothioneine.

Keywords: mycology, Cascadia fungi, fungal bioluminescence, 3-hydroxyhispidin, Amanita phalloides, chitin thermodynamics, ergothioneine, mycorrhizal glomalin
