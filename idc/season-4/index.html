Higher Ground 4.01 — “The Second Set of Keys”

Inez presents the symbolic emerald-ribboned keys of cooperative victory, only for the tenants to discover none of the locks match.

INEZ Welcome to year two of the Green Cathedral Cooperative. These keys represent our shared sovereignty. They open the front oak doors, the main meeting floor, the woodcraft workshop, and the courtyard garden gate. RAFI, a new tenant who builds bespoke acoustic instruments, takes his key and walks immediately to the accessible side entrance on Elm Street. He inserts the key into the heavy mortise lock. It turns freely in an endless, loose circle without catching the latch. RAFI The side door is spinning. JALEN walks to the supply room door behind the kitchen. He inserts his key. It doesn't even enter the keyway. JALEN The supply room keyway is thirty percent too narrow. SADE walks to the tall oak childcare storage armoire. Her key goes in halfway and jams tight. SADE Mine is wedged. Who cut these?

Deeper State 4.01 — “The Emergency Contact Is a Committee”

Priya and Owen face an automated telephone nightmare at 2:13 A.M. as three regional utility routes fail simultaneously.

INT. PRIYA'S OFFICE — 2:13 A.M. One red phone rings beneath seven laminated cards: PRIMARY CONTACT. ALTERNATE CONTACT. LOCAL CONTACT. CUSTODY CONTACT. ACCESS CONTACT. LABOR CONTACT. CONTACT-GOVERNANCE CONTACT. Priya answers. AUTOMATED VOICE For a service interruption, press one. To determine who is authorized to press one, press two. Priya presses one. AUTOMATED VOICE This action requires confirmation from the contact-governance contact. Across the room, Owen opens a binder. OWEN The contact-governance contact is a committee. The red phone keeps ringing in Priya's hand.

The Cancellation Department 4.01 — “Return Address Unknown”

Eve, Sol, and Nine unpack freight crates filled with decommissioned props and an unauthorized scene continuing beyond the finale.

INT. DECOMMISSIONED CARGO DOCK — MORNING EVE cuts through three layers of black reinforced vinyl shipping wrap with an industrial utility blade. A six-foot-tall wooden hand-painted station clock from the series finale emerges. Its hands are welded permanently to 4:48. SOL We closed that station nine weeks ago. We signed the export manifest. NINE Someone returned it without an airbill. A small thermal printer on the freight desk chatters. It spits out a strip of lavender register paper: RETURN ACCEPTED UNDER UNFINISHED CLAUSE 14. REASON: THE AUDIENCE IS STILL INSIDE THE PLATFORM. EVE Clause fourteen was struck in nineteen ninety-eight. NINE The clock doesn't know that. It's still ticking.

Uncommon Side Effects 4.04 — “The Authorized Ghost”

Nia and Kiva discover an automated legacy service account quietly validating transformations five years after the regulator retired.

INT. RECORDS VAULT TERMINAL ALCOVE — 2:40 A.M. A luminous test card appears on the frosted glass screen: [PROJECTION CARD: S4Q-010 — RETIRED SERVICE ACCOUNT ROUTE DETECTION] PURPOSE: Evaluate whether defunct municipal credentials retain automated signing power. STATUS: ACTIVE QUERY. NIA Service account 409-Delta has submitted twelve thousand transformation confirmations since March. KIVA Who is 409-Delta? ELI Arthur Vance. He retired to Maine in twenty twenty-one. He spends his mornings building cedar birdhouses. NIA Arthur's birdhouses must be extraordinary, because his digital signature just approved three regional pharmaceutical registries at twelve seconds past midnight.
