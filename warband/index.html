The Faith of the Prism

When the abyss opened and the radiation came, the faithful did not burn — they bloomed. Crystal erupted from bone, glyphs seared themselves into skin, and the light that poured out was every color at once. They call it a blessing. They march to spread it.

"Refracted, we are made whole."

The Procession is a carnival of the transfigured: revelers in cracked porcelain masks, acolytes swinging laser-censers, glow-touched horrors, and the crowned saints who lead them — all bound by the Nine Threads of VORATH.
