Epistemic Integrity & Classroom Safety

How We Label What You Read

Every resource in NorthStar Commons states clearly whether it is an illustrated story, a hands-on engineering model, an aspirational civic-art synthesis, or a literature review.

Contained Family Lanes

Safe for Young Readers

Our family reading room and 50-book Little Light Library contain zero ad networks, zero social feeds, and zero autoplay video embeds, with readable print controls for shared bedtime or classroom reading.

Sourced vs. Proposed

Transparent Evidence Grades

Research monographs distinguish verified equations and peer-reviewed citations from theoretical designs (Invention Forge) and aspirational civic art (Bill of Rights v2, not enacted law).

Educational, Not Prescriptive

Clinical & Botanical Guardrails

Botanical, nutritional, and biomedical syntheses include toxic-lookalike warnings, CYP3A4 transplant guardrails, and clear reminders to consult a licensed physician or renal dietitian before changing care.
