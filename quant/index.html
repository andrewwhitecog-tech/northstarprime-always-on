01.

Score the estimate.

Binary Brier score = (probability − outcome)², where Yes is 1 and No is 0. Scores range from 0 to 1; smaller is better. Unresolved events have no score. NWS verification reference.

02.

Keep the comparison honest.

One case cannot establish skill. Evaluate every event in a fixed cohort, retain uncertain or void outcomes, and test future held-out cases. Adjusted estimates for separate events do not form a joint probability distribution.
