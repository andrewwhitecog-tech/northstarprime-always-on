# Last Call Espresso Affogato for Two

**Section:** After Dark / Date Night  
**Rating:** R (18+)  
**Yield:** 2 servings (2 scoops gelato + 2 shots espresso)  
**Time:** 10 minutes  

**Adult tone note:** Dessert that arrives late and overdressed in espresso—hot meets cold, spoons optional. Playful last-call cabaret; consensual adults; not pornographic. Caffeine is a stimulant in the ordinary food sense, not a sexual-performance claim.

**medical_review_status:** not clinician-reviewed; renal limits require clinician/dietitian

## Ingredients

- 2 large scoops (about 1 cup / 150 g total) vanilla gelato or ice cream  
- 2 shots (about 2 oz / 60 ml total) hot freshly pulled espresso **or** 1/2 cup (120 ml) very strong hot coffee  
- Optional: 1 Tbsp (15 ml) coffee liqueur, divided (adults)  
- Optional: dark chocolate shavings or cocoa for dusting  

## Steps

1. Chill two small bowls or glasses in the freezer 5–10 minutes.  
2. Brew espresso (or strong coffee) so it is hot and ready.  
3. Place one scoop of gelato in each glass.  
4. Optional: drizzle a little coffee liqueur over each scoop.  
5. Pour one hot shot (or half the coffee) over each scoop at the table so the melt happens in real time.  
6. Dust with chocolate if using. Serve immediately—this is a moment, not a make-ahead.

## Allergen notes

Contains **milk** (gelato/ice cream). Some gelatos contain **eggs**; chocolate may contain **soy** or **tree nut** cross-contact—check labels. Coffee/espresso is caffeine-containing.

## Safe storage / reheat

- Affogato is made to order; do not store assembled.  
- Keep ice cream frozen at 0°F (−18°C) or below per usual frozen-dessert practice.  
- Leftover espresso: refrigerate promptly; use within a day or discard. If incorporating leftovers into another hot dish later, reheat that dish to **165°F (74°C)**.  
- USDA FSIS leftovers: https://www.fsis.usda.gov/food-safety/safe-food-handling-and-preparation/food-safety-basics/leftovers-and-food-safety  
- Safe temperature chart: https://www.fsis.usda.gov/food-safety/safe-food-handling-and-preparation/food-safety-basics/safe-temperature-chart
