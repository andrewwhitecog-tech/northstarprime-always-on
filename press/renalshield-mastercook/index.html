Official Release

NorthStar Prime Releases Free MasterCook Kidney Kitchen & RenalShield 7-Hour Chairside Companion to Support Dialysis Community

SEATTLE, WA & GLOBAL — September 22, 2026 — NorthStar Prime today announced the public release of two landmark freeware platforms created specifically for people living with End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD) and Chronic Kidney Disease (CKD): the Digital MasterCook Book: The Alchemical Kidney Kitchen and the RenalShield Chairside Care Companion.

More than 800,000 individuals in the United States alone live with irreversible kidney failure, requiring life-sustaining dialysis three times weekly or kidney transplantation. For decades, kidney diets have been plagued by a pervasive myth: that food must be bland, boiled, and devoid of texture or crunch to be safe. At the same time, patients face a silent, lethal grocery hazard: inorganic phosphate food additives.

"Dialysis isn’t a 45-minute gym session—it’s a 7-hour haul portal-to-portal. And you’re constantly told what you can’t eat, can’t drink, and can’t do. We built RenalShield and the MasterCook Grimoire from the inside out: real crunch, real savory pan reductions without chemical salts, and lived-experience strategies for that long day in the chair."
— Andre White Jr., Founder & Lead Builder, NorthStar Prime

Three Core Innovations

  1. The Inorganic Phosphate Additive Screener: Unlike natural organic phosphorus found in whole meats and plants (which is only ~40–60% absorbed by the human body), industrial inorganic phosphate additives (*sodium tripolyphosphate*, *dicalcium phosphate*, *phosphoric acid*) are absorbed at an alarming 90–100% rate, driving dangerous vascular calcification. RenalShield’s photo and label screener catches these additives in 3 seconds and serves up whole-food "Chef Swaps".
  2. The Crisping Alchemy Lab (200+ Tested Freeware Recipes): Through controlled culinary science—cornstarch-egg white micro-dredging, high-heat sears, and apple cider vinegar deglazes—the MasterCook collection produces shattering, craggy cutlets and comforting heritage dishes with zero added artificial phosphates and ultra-low sodium.
  3. The Chairside 7-Hour Circadian Transmission & Care Kit: Tailored specifically for clinic days, featuring an SVG circadian session arc (Arrival, The Long Middle, Departure), audio soundscapes from IDR Records, and non-fluid thirst tamers (frozen sour grapes, tart lemon swabs) to manage severe dry mouth without exceeding dangerous fluid restrictions.
