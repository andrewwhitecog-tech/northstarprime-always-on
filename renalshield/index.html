RenalShield

A clearer way to compare the label in your hand. RenalShield helps people living with CKD or dialysis—and the people who cook beside them—notice sodium, potassium, phosphorus, and ingredient patterns without pretending one renal diet fits every body.

Open RenalShield — free Explore the cookbook archive
Screen the package

Paste an ingredient list or nutrition label and compare the information with targets supplied by your care team.

Spot hidden signals

Look for phosphate additives, potassium additives, sodium load, and other details that are easy to miss in small print.

Keep it private

No login is required. Personal logs and targets stay in the browser unless you choose to export a private backup.

Important: RenalShield is a patient-built educational tool, not medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, or a substitute for your nephrologist or renal dietitian. Individual needs vary with labs, medications, kidney function, dialysis modality, and the plan set by your care team.
