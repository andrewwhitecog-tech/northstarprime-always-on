VORATH INCURSION

A top-down cover shooter. The Shadow legion pushes through the tribunal ruins in waves. Snap to cover behind the marble blocks, pop out to fire, and survive.

Signature mechanics: hold Space near a block to lock into cover (bullets are blocked while tucked); roadie-run with Shift for a burst of speed; and the active reload — when you reload, a needle sweeps a bar. Tap R again in the gold slice for a perfect reload (damage boost + instant), the green slice for a fast reload, or miss and it jams for a beat.
