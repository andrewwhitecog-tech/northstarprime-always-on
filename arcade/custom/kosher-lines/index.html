How the courier rehearsal works

Choose the no-clock chapter ride. Open the route, then find the amber dispatch seal in the west court, behind and left of the starting mosaic. Brake inside it and start the three-count. Stay on the seal until the signal changes to acid green, then skate through the numbered light frames in order, with your wheels on the ground. The lit inlays show the route. T, or controller Start, opens the course and starts it when you are ready at dispatch.

A false start, bail, skipped relay, ride reset, time-out or leaving the marked route for more than two seconds ends the attempt. Brake at the dispatch seal to retry. Pausing freezes ride time. A clean delivery earns a relay crest fitted to your board and saves your best time on this device: gold within 18 seconds, silver within 28, bronze for any delivery within 60. These are local game rewards, with no online rank or currency payout.

    Controls & the five-feature line

    W / ↑ pushes; S / ↓ brakes; A / D steers. Hold Space to crouch, then release for a higher ollie. Tap F for kickflip, G for heelflip, H for shove-it, J for 180, K for tre flip and U for Nac flip while airborne. Catch the board before you land. R drops back in; P or Escape pauses.

    Jump onto a gold rail to lock a grind automatically. A / D corrects balance; ollie off to link another trick. Hold M for a manual. Unbanked points are at risk until you roll clean for two seconds.

    The line runs in order: build speed through the first lamp; grind the left ledge for a moment; ollie the central funbox; clear the descending stair set; ride up the far quarter and land its return. A bail resets the current line, not your saved career.

    Drag the view to orbit the chase camera. On a controller: left stick steers, right trigger pushes, left trigger brakes; A charges an ollie; B flips, X shoves, Y tre-flips, LB performs a Nac and RB manuals. Touch buttons support simultaneous holds. For a charged trick, hold its painted button together with Ollie, then release both fingers. You can also tap a trick while airborne.
