COLOR 01 RESTLESS LIGHT At dawn, Iri carried the prism lantern through Paleglass City, waking a little color in every window. But one ribbon of light wriggled free. "Please choose a color," Iri said. The ribbon flashed every color at once and zipped around the corner.

COLOR 10 TOO MANY NAMES The townspeople crowded the doorway. "It is red." "It is blue." "It is gold." Everyone held tightly to the color they had seen. The room grew noisy. The ribbon grew thin. Iri lowered her lantern and listened until every voice had finished.

COLOR 11 BETWEEN THE NAMES "Every name tells something true," Iri said. "But no name tells everything." Together, the people opened the prism room to the sky. They built no cage and painted no line. They left space between the names for color to move.

COLOR 12 EVERYWHERE At sunrise, the restless ribbon poured through Paleglass City - red and copper, orange and marigold, green and teal, blue and violet, shadow and shine. It returned to Iri's lantern only long enough to light the next window. A color could have a name, Iri knew, without having only one home.
