Docket DC-001

The Case of the Missing Sequence

Aster Vale built a public identity around a five-part creative ritual. Then Door Five vanished. The source file is intact. The hash is unchanged. Yet the fifth movement has become a person-sized negative space inside the Court of Unwoven Reality.

The claimAster calls it theft: somebody removed the part that made the ritual whole.
The problemThe evidence permits three explanations—intrusion, entropy, or an instruction Aster no longer wants to remember giving.

The horror is procedural, not monstrous. Nothing chases the witness. A clerk files the transcript. That is the mechanism—and it is worse.

The Court cannot restore what the record cannot prove. It can, however, authorize a reconstruction whose seams remain visible. The question is whether Aster wants continuity badly enough to accept a future that refuses to impersonate the past.
