# Barely Dressed Burrata with Balsamic Kiss

**Section:** After Dark / Date Night  
**Rating:** R (18+)  
**Yield:** 2 appetizer servings (1 large burrata + tomatoes)  
**Time:** 15 minutes  

**Adult tone note:** Minimal clothing for the cheese—olive oil, cracked pepper, a balsamic kiss. Suggestive plating language only; consensual adults; not pornographic.

**medical_review_status:** not clinician-reviewed; renal limits require clinician/dietitian

## Ingredients

- 1 ball (8 oz / 225 g) burrata, drained  
- 8 oz (225 g) ripe cherry or heirloom tomatoes, halved or sliced  
- 2 Tbsp (30 ml) extra-virgin olive oil  
- 1 Tbsp (15 ml) balsamic glaze (or reduce 3 Tbsp balsamic vinegar to syrupy)  
- Flaky salt and freshly cracked black pepper  
- Handful fresh basil leaves  
- Optional: 4 slices toasted baguette or focaccia for scooping  

## Steps

1. Arrange tomatoes on a shared platter; drizzle with half the olive oil and a pinch of salt.  
2. Nestle the burrata in the center. Score the top lightly so it opens invitingly when you dig in.  
3. Drizzle remaining oil and balsamic glaze over cheese and tomatoes.  
4. Crack pepper generously; tear basil over the top.  
5. Serve at cool room temperature with toast. Two forks. No rush.

## Allergen notes

Contains **milk** (burrata). Bread may contain **wheat/gluten**, **soy**, or **sesame**. Basil and tomatoes are typically free of top allergens; confirm preferences.

## Safe storage / reheat

- Best same day once opened. Leftover burrata: refrigerate promptly in a covered container; use within 1–2 days.  
- Do not leave soft cheese at room temperature > 2 hours (1 hour if ambient > 90°F / 32°C).  
- Toast leftovers: reheat bread until hot; cheese is typically eaten cold/cool—if combining into a warm dish later, heat leftovers to **165°F (74°C)**.  
- USDA FSIS leftovers / 2-hour rule: https://www.fsis.usda.gov/food-safety/safe-food-handling-and-preparation/food-safety-basics/leftovers-and-food-safety  
- Safe temperature chart: https://www.fsis.usda.gov/food-safety/safe-food-handling-and-preparation/food-safety-basics/safe-temperature-chart
