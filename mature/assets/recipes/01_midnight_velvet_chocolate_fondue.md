# Midnight Velvet Chocolate Fondue for Two

**Section:** After Dark / Date Night  
**Rating:** R (18+)  
**Yield:** 2 servings (about 1 cup / 240 ml fondue + fruit and cake for dipping)  
**Time:** 20 minutes active; 25 minutes total  

**Adult tone note:** Playful cabaret date-night energy—suggestive, consensual, and clothed in chocolate. Not pornographic; keep the flirting at the table.

**medical_review_status:** not clinician-reviewed; renal limits require clinician/dietitian

## Ingredients

- 6 oz (170 g) good dark chocolate (60–70% cacao), chopped  
- 1/2 cup (120 ml) heavy cream  
- 1 Tbsp (14 g) unsalted butter  
- 1 tsp (5 ml) pure vanilla extract  
- Pinch of fine salt  
- 1 Tbsp (15 ml) orange liqueur **or** 1 tsp orange zest (optional, adults only)  
- For dipping: 1 cup (150 g) strawberries, hulled; 1 banana, sliced; 4 oz (115 g) pound cake or soft pretzels, cubed  

## Equipment

Small saucepan or double boiler, fondue pot or heatproof bowl, skewers or forks

## Steps

1. Pat fruit dry so the chocolate clings instead of sliding off in a sultry puddle.  
2. Warm cream and butter in a small saucepan over low heat until steaming—do not boil.  
3. Remove from heat. Add chopped chocolate; let sit 1 minute, then stir until glossy and smooth.  
4. Stir in vanilla, salt, and optional orange liqueur or zest. Transfer to a fondue pot or bowl set over a candle warmer / low heat so it stays pourable.  
5. Arrange fruit and cake on a shared plate. Dip, share, and linger. Keep skewers out of mouths between dips for courtesy.

## Allergen notes

Contains **milk** and **soy** (common in chocolate). Cake/pretzels may contain **wheat/gluten**, **eggs**, and **tree nuts** depending on brand—check labels. Banana and strawberries are usually safe for those allergens but confirm household preferences.

## Safe storage / reheat

- Leftover fondue: cool, cover, refrigerate ≤ 3–4 days.  
- Reheat gently over low heat or short microwave bursts, stirring until smooth; do not boil.  
- Cut fruit: refrigerate promptly; discard cut fruit left at room temperature > 2 hours (1 hour if > 90°F / 32°C).  
- Guidance: USDA FSIS leftovers and the 2-hour rule — https://www.fsis.usda.gov/food-safety/safe-food-handling-and-preparation/food-safety-basics/leftovers-and-food-safety

## Temperature citations

No raw meat/poultry. For any leftover cake reheated with fondue later, reheat leftovers to **165°F (74°C)** per USDA FSIS: https://www.fsis.usda.gov/food-safety/safe-food-handling-and-preparation/food-safety-basics/safe-temperature-chart
