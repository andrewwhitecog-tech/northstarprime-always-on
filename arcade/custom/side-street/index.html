HOW IT WORKS

Make the corners count.

  1. Move one crossing at a time: up, down, left or right.
  2. Visit the numbered stops in order. Each crossing can be used once.
  3. A change of direction counts as one turn. The first step costs no turns.
  4. Finish at home with exactly the target number. You don’t need to cover the whole map.

Tap an earlier part of your route to undo back to it. Wrong routes are easy to change.

Keyboard & controller

Tab to the map. Arrow keys move the focus; Enter or Space adds a crossing. Backspace undoes a step while the map has focus.

With a standard gamepad: D-pad moves focus, A chooses, B undoes. Focus the map first. Physical gamepad testing is still pending.
