SOURCE DISCIPLINE

No mystery meat data.

The atlas links every material claim to its source. Official pages are selected manually, not bulk-extracted. It does not scrape J! Archive or Sony, and it does not treat a public API mirror as permission to republish copyrighted clue text. Open entity data and owner-authorized exports stay separately identified.
