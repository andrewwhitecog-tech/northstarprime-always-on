# Candlelight Kiss Champagne Pasta

**Section:** After Dark / Date Night  
**Rating:** R (18+)  
**Yield:** 2 generous servings (~10 oz / 280 g cooked pasta + sauce)  
**Time:** 10 minutes prep; 20 minutes cook; ~30 minutes total  

**Adult tone note:** Bubbles in the pan, steam in the air, forks that accidentally (or not) meet. Suggestive cabaret pasta for two; consensual adults; not pornographic.

**medical_review_status:** not clinician-reviewed; renal limits require clinician/dietitian

## Ingredients

- 8 oz (225 g) dried spaghetti or linguine  
- 2 Tbsp (28 g) unsalted butter  
- 1 Tbsp (15 ml) olive oil  
- 3 garlic cloves, thinly sliced  
- Pinch of red pepper flakes (optional)  
- 1/2 cup (120 ml) dry sparkling wine or champagne (alcohol cooks down; adults)  
- 1/2 cup (120 ml) heavy cream  
- 1/2 cup (40 g) finely grated Parmesan  
- Zest of 1/2 lemon + 1 tsp lemon juice  
- Kosher salt for pasta water; black pepper to finish  
- 2 Tbsp chopped parsley  

## Steps

1. Bring a large pot of well-salted water to a boil. Cook pasta until just shy of al dente per package timing; reserve 1 cup (240 ml) pasta water before draining.  
2. Meanwhile, in a wide skillet over medium heat, warm butter and olive oil. Add garlic and optional pepper flakes; cook until garlic is fragrant and pale gold, ~1–2 minutes—do not burn.  
3. Pour in sparkling wine; simmer until reduced by about half, ~2–3 minutes.  
4. Stir in cream; simmer gently 1–2 minutes until slightly thickened.  
5. Add drained pasta to the skillet with Parmesan, lemon zest, lemon juice, and a splash of pasta water. Toss vigorously until glossy and clinging; add more pasta water as needed.  
6. Season with black pepper. Plate in shallow bowls; finish with parsley. Eat while it’s still steaming enough to fog the candlelight.

## Allergen notes

Contains **wheat/gluten** (pasta), **milk** (butter, cream, Parmesan). Parmesan is dairy; some aged cheeses are lower-lactose but still **milk** allergen. No eggs unless your pasta brand includes them—check label.

## Safe storage / reheat

- Refrigerate leftovers within 2 hours; use within 3–4 days.  
- Reheat until steaming hot throughout; leftovers to **165°F (74°C)**. Add a splash of water or cream when reheating so the sauce loosens.
