DOOR 01 THE LISTENING GARDEN After the storm, Jun found twelve doors standing in a circle inside the old glasshouse. Every door opened inward. Beyond them waited a moth, a fox, an otter, an owl, a tortoise, a frog, a porcupine, a bat, a sand cat, and a seal. "A door should know both directions," Jun said.

DOOR 12 RETURNING / OPEN BOTH WAYS Two doors remained: one for Jun's town, and one for any traveler the garden had yet to meet. Jun left both unlatched. This garden was not for keeping anyone. It was a place for listening until the right direction became clear. By dawn, all twelve doors opened outward - and every one could open inward again.
