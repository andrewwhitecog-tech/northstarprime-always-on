How we want this to work.

Our business priority is paid work for organizations with a real need and an appropriate budget. The aim is to help sustain public culture and knowledge without relying on people who cannot afford another subscription.

Do I need to join or sign in?

No. Public listening, fifty-one arcade games, films, and the Mystery School entry experience are completely open to guests. Account availability is separate from free access.

Is paid membership available?

Allocation keys and patron privileges open during scheduled release cycles. Guest mode remains permanently available for all games, soundscapes, films, and the Mystery School portal with zero subscriptions required.

Does free access include commercial reuse?

No. Free viewing or listening does not grant a commercial license. Check each work's stated terms, or contact us about a specific use.

Can an organization license or support a collection?

Yes, you can ask about a named collection and use. Availability, rights, format, accessibility, support and price are reviewed in a written proposal before any agreement.
