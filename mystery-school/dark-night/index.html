The Smallest Reversible Crossing

  1. Sit somewhere ordinary: A kitchen chair, a quiet corner, feet on the floor. Verify physical safety.
  2. Observe the looping script: Name the thought that woke you up. Say it in one neutral sentence.
  3. Separate fact from prediction: The fact is what occurred today. The prediction is what your anxiety is writing for tomorrow.
  4. Return to the waking world: Drink a glass of cold water. Touch one cool surface. Leave the door unlocked for tomorrow's work.
