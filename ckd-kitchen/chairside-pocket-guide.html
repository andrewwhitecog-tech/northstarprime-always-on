Cut or fold for your clinic tote

✦ 0-Fluid Thirst Tamers

  • Frozen Sour Grapes: Keep 2–3 frozen in a small insulated flask. Sub-zero cold numbs palate nerve endings; natural malic acid stimulates salivary flow with negligible liquid volume.
  • Tart Lemon Spritz / Swab: Rub a fresh lemon slice over gums or use a cold spray mist on the tongue. Satisfies acute thirst instantly.
  • Sugar-Free Sour Mints: Zero-phosphate spearmint or lemon drops stimulate saliva for 20+ minutes during the long middle hours.
  • Chilled Cucumber Discs: Peeled cucumber slices (peeling removes excess potassium) provide crisp, refreshing moisture.
Clinic Tip: When your interdialytic fluid target is 1,000 mL/day, drinking water just creates fluid gain and cramping. Non-fluid oral stimulation stops dry mouth at the nerve level.

The Grocery Phosphate Trap

  • Check the Ingredient List: Reject anything containing words ending in PHOSPHATE or PHOSPHORIC (*sodium tripolyphosphate*, *dicalcium phosphate*).
  • Why it matters: Added inorganic phosphates absorb at 90–100% vs. only 40–60% for natural meat/plant phosphorus.
  • Avoid "Lite Salt" / Salt Substitute: These replace sodium with potassium chloride, which can spike serum potassium dangerously fast.

✦ Safe Chairside Crunch

  • Herb-Dusted Air Popcorn: Tossed with smoked paprika and dried thyme (5 mg sodium, 0 potassium chloride).
  • Puffed Rice & Cream Cheese: Low-sodium rice cakes with a light spread of cream cheese and fresh chopped dill.
  • Washout Recovery Box: Chilled sliced roasted chicken cutlets or hard-boiled egg whites (no yolk) waiting for the ride home.
