# Between the Sheets Steak au Poivre for Two

**Section:** After Dark / Date Night  
**Rating:** R (18+)  
**Yield:** 2 servings (2 steaks + ~3/4 cup / 180 ml peppercorn cream sauce)  
**Time:** 15 minutes prep; 25 minutes cook; ~40 minutes total  

**Adult tone note:** Classic steakhouse seduction—pepper heat, cream silk, shared plate. Suggestive cabaret tone; consensual adults only; not pornographic.

**medical_review_status:** not clinician-reviewed; renal limits require clinician/dietitian

## Ingredients

- 2 beef strip steaks or filet mignon, about 8 oz (225 g) each, ~1–1½ in (2.5–4 cm) thick  
- 1½ tsp (9 g) kosher salt, divided as needed  
- 2 Tbsp (20 g) coarsely cracked black peppercorns  
- 1 Tbsp (15 ml) neutral oil (canola or grapeseed)  
- 1 Tbsp (14 g) unsalted butter  
- 1 small shallot, minced (~2 Tbsp / 20 g)  
- 1/4 cup (60 ml) brandy or cognac (optional flambé; adults; omit if preferred)  
- 1/2 cup (120 ml) beef broth (low-sodium if you prefer)  
- 1/3 cup (80 ml) heavy cream  
- 1 tsp (5 ml) Dijon mustard  
- Chopped parsley for finish (optional)  

## Steps

1. Pat steaks dry. Season lightly with salt. Press cracked pepper into both sides. Rest 10 minutes at cool room temp while you prep sauce ingredients.  
2. Heat a heavy skillet over medium-high until hot. Add oil, then steaks. Sear without moving until a deep crust forms, ~3–5 minutes per side depending on thickness.  
3. Check doneness with a food thermometer in the thickest part. For **steaks, USDA FSIS: cook to at least 145°F (63°C) and rest 3 minutes**. Prefer medium? Aim near that floor and rest.  
4. Transfer steaks to a warm plate; tent loosely with foil. Rest ≥ 3 minutes.  
5. Lower heat to medium. Add butter and shallot; cook until soft and fragrant, ~1–2 minutes.  
6. Optional: off heat, add brandy carefully; return to heat and simmer until nearly evaporated (never pour spirit from an open bottle over flame).  
7. Add broth; simmer 2–3 minutes to reduce slightly. Stir in cream and Dijon; simmer until sauce coats a spoon, ~2–3 minutes. Taste; adjust salt sparingly.  
8. Spoon sauce over rested steaks. Share the plate; pass the rest of the wine if that’s your scene.

## Allergen notes

Contains **milk** (butter, cream). Broth may contain **soy** or **wheat**—check label. Mustard is usually fine; confirm for mustard allergy. No shellfish/eggs unless your broth blend includes them.

## Safe storage / reheat

- Refrigerate leftovers within 2 hours in shallow containers; use within 3–4 days.  
- Reheat leftovers to **165°F (74°C)** until steaming hot.
