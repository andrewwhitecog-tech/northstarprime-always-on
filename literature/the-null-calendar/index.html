The Null
Calendar

The first confirmed contact event does not arrive from the sky. It appears inside humanity's archives—and one missing date forces scientists, clergy, courts, families, and governments to decide whether truth can survive the institutions built to contain it.

The record has not ended.

Built as a documentary thriller, The Null Calendar moves through candidate-event notices, verification ladders, hearings, testimony, correction logs, school records, and the people living beneath those systems. Its central question is not whether humanity is special. It is whether humanity can keep an honest record when the answer becomes inconvenient.

Fiction boundary: the institutions, records, and disclosure scenario are invented. The novel does not present unsupported alien-disclosure claims as fact.
