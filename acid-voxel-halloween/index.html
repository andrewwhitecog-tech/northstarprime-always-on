12 Flagship Plates Across All Three Waves

#01 [E01] · Wave 1 · Hero / Macro Eye (Rule 29)

The Voxel Labyrinth Eye

Peer into the cubic abyss. This ultra-detailed macro eye illustration reveals an isometric 3D voxel Halloween labyrinth built from glowing jack-o'-lantern blocks, iridescent rainbow bismuth step-crystals, neon UV-blacklight mycelium, and crying obsidian around a blood-moon eclipse pupil.

#02 [M01] · Wave 1 · Voxel Mob

Acid-Spore Jack-o'-Bomber

A mutated four-legged blocky voxel creeper-golem fused with a melting UV-blacklight jack-o'-lantern, iridescent bismuth crystal armor, bioluminescent mushrooms, and a blazing prismatic cosmic Eye portal inside its hollow chest grid.

#09 [L01] · Wave 1 · Voxel Legend

The Fog Glitch Apparition

Emerging from a haunted neon autumn forest, the legendary white-eyed cubic apparition dissolves into chromatic-aberration voxel blocks, iridescent bismuth shards, and a blazing third-eye cosmic portal halo.

#13 [B02] · Wave 1 · Voxel Boss

Tri-Ocular Acid Wither Boss

Three crying-obsidian and bismuth voxel skulls blaze in neon magenta, electric cyan, and acid-lime soul flames above a colossal cosmic Eye singularity vortex pulling in glowing mushrooms and cubic debris.

#17 [E02] · Wave 2 · Hero / Macro Eye (Rule 29)

The Bismuth Redstone Eclipse Eye

An extreme macro close-up of a colossal supernatural Eye forged from concentric rings of glowing redstone circuitry cubes, rainbow bismuth step-crystals, carved jack-o'-lantern blocks, and a vertical obsidian eclipse pupil reflecting a miniature floating voxel castle.

#18 [M05] · Wave 2 · Voxel Mob

Psychedelic Magma-Slime Matriarch

A colossal tiered translucent cubic slime-magma monster whose interlocking gelatinous voxel layers reveal a floating cosmic Eye portal core, carved jack-o'-lantern cubes, bismuth crystals, and neon UV lava drips.

#28 [B05] · Wave 2 · Voxel Boss

Elder Prism Sea-Guardian Boss

Armored in ancient sea-lantern cubes and extending twelve neon-orange crystalline spikes, this colossal aquatic voxel boss fires a spiraling kaleidoscope laser beam from its giant central cosmic Eye.

#30 [B07] · Wave 2 · Voxel Boss

Eightfold Pantheon Solar Archon

Framed by an octagonal wheel of stained-glass voxel monoliths and eight levitating elemental pumpkin-crystal masks, this cosmic cubic seraph radiates psychedelic aurora light from its central cosmic Eye eclipse.

#33 [E03] · Wave 3 · Hero / Macro Eye (Rule 29)

The Visceral Eldritch Flesh-Labyrinth Eye

An extreme macro close-up of a colossal supernatural Eldritch Voxel Eye framed by crying-obsidian cubes and bone teeth, where the iris is a biomechanical abyss of weeping crimson flesh-blocks, skeletal rib-arches, rainbow bismuth fangs, and UV tentacles converging on an acid-lime black-hole pupil.

#34 [G01] · Wave 3 · Acid-Gore Voxel Horror

Flayed Flesh-Host Parasite

A horrifying mutated four-legged cubic horror whose peeled mossy pumpkin skin exposes jagged white bone ribs, a dripping neon-crimson anatomical voxel heart, eye-studded ultraviolet tentacles, and a blazing vertical Cosmic Eye chest portal framed by bismuth fangs.

#41 [X01] · Wave 3 · Eldritch Voxel Outer-God

The All-In-One Gate

A supernatural cosmic outer-god formed from thirteen interlocking iridescent voxel orbs, anatomical eyeballs, and carved pumpkin moons fused by weeping crimson protoplasmic tendrils and impossible Escher staircases around a massive Cosmic Eye portal.

#43 [X03] · Wave 3 · Eldritch Voxel Outer-God

Abyssal Leviathan Dreamer

A monumental cephalopod-dragon voxel titan forged from bioluminescent deep-trench prismarine cubes, exposed crimson gill-ribs, tattered stained-glass wings, and a neon tentacle beard rising over non-Euclidean sunken temple blocks with a galaxy Eye brow portal.
