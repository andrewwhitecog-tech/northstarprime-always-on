Autonomous deep-field marine containment simulation. Micro-organisms feed, reproduce, mutate, and hunt in real time under hydrothermal pressure, oxygen gradients, and genetic selection.

  3. Dive the trench. Channer Sonar paints the black with echoes — life answers in clicks and blooms.

  5. Reef shallows. Vorathic Reef Tank opens the warm end of the family — luminous, intimate, and still not tame.
