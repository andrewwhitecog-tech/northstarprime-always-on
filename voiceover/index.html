What you get

  • A natural-sounding narration of your script, in the tone you pick: warm, upbeat, calm, confident or plain-spoken.
  • Your brand, product and place names pronounced the way you tell us, checked word by word against your notes.
  • A clean WAV file (48 kHz) plus an MP3 copy, with no music and no background noise.
  • Split files, one per paragraph or scene, on Standard and Plus, so your editor can place lines on the timeline.

How it works

  1. Email your script, tone, names to pronounce and deadline. We reply with the package and price.
  2. Within 24 hours we send a short test line (about 10 seconds) so you approve the voice before the full read.
  3. You get the full track within 48 hours, and can ask for changes within 7 days.

Good to know

  • This voice is AI-generated. We produce it on a paid, commercial-licensed AI voice plan, then a person listens to and checks every line. Tell your audience if your platform or local rules ask you to.
  • We never copy or imitate a real person’s voice, a celebrity or a public figure.
  • You must own the script or have the right to use it. We don’t read hateful, deceptive, medical-claim or political-ad scripts.
  • Revisions cover pronunciation, pace, emphasis and tone. Mistakes we made are fixed free and don’t use a round. Rewriting the script after delivery is a new read.
  • If you reject the test line and we can’t offer a voice you approve before the full read, you get a full refund. After the full read is delivered and accepted, the order is final.
  • Use the finished track in your own business videos, ads, websites, podcasts and phone system. Reselling the audio as a voice pack, sample library or stock audio is not included.
  • Add-on: a voiceover can be added to a Shorts editing order.
