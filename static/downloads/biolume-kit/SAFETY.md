# SAFETY — BIOLUME Festival Wearable

## Product framing (mandatory)

**BIOLUME is living-light / festival / costume / biofeedback-aesthetic art.**

It is **NOT**:

- a medical device
- a diagnostic tool
- a blood-pressure, heart-rate, dialysis, or vital-sign monitor
- a treatment, therapy, or clinical early-warning system

Do **not** claim that this wearable detects, predicts, prevents, or treats any medical condition. Do **not** use words like “clinical,” “FDA,” “dialysis crisis,” “BP drop,” or “patient safety device” in store listings, ads, or packaging.

Onboard sensors (motion, temperature, etc.) may drive **aesthetic** light reactions. That is mood / art / self-expression — not clinical biofeedback therapy and not a regulated health claim.

If you build a personal experiment for yourself, keep it personal. **Commerce copy stays art-only.**

## Lithium polymer (LiPo) care

- Use **protected** cells from reputable suppliers (see BOM Adafruit links).
- Charge only with a proper LiPo charger (e.g. Adafruit #1304); never with a random USB cable into the bare cell.
- Do not puncture, crush, fold, or short the pack. Helmet foam and pendant pockets need a hard or semi-rigid barrier around the cell.
- Do not ship overcharged packs; follow lithium battery shipping regulations for your carrier and country.
- Do not leave charging unattended on flammable costume materials.
- If a pack swells, smells, or heats abnormally: disconnect power, isolate outdoors on a non-flammable surface, and dispose per local battery rules.
- Skin-worn builds: thermal + puncture protection between cell and body is required.

---

## Electrical / NeoPixel

- Large strips draw significant current — use **battery / VOUT**, not USB alone, for more than a handful of pixels.
- Observe polarity; reverse 5V can kill the strip or board.
- Insulate solder joints; sweat and rain + exposed pads = shorts.
- Disconnect battery when soldering or modifying wiring.

---

## Photosensitivity / Festival Strobe

**Mode 6 — Festival Strobe** is a high-energy flashing pattern.

- Can trigger discomfort or seizures in people with photosensitive epilepsy.
- Warn companions; avoid pointing strobes at faces at close range.
- Prefer quieter modes (BIOLUME, Aurora, Sacred Pulse) for chill / indoor / mixed crowds.
- Slide switch to **quiet** brightness when in doubt.

---

## Resin / materials (if you cast a diffuser)

- Fully cure epoxy / UV resin before skin contact. Uncured epoxy can sensitize skin.
- Work with gloves and ventilation per resin MSDS.
- Original geometry / IP only for commercial shells.
