How to read a million-page room

A name is not a verdict

A contact entry can show reachability. A flight log can show recorded travel. A photograph can show co-presence. None, alone, establishes knowledge of or participation in abuse.

An indictment is not a conviction

Charging papers establish what prosecutors alleged and intended to prove. Keep them labeled as allegations unless a plea, verdict, or finding resolves them.

A redaction is not a confession

Victim privacy, grand-jury secrecy, sealed orders, personal data, and illegal material create lawful reasons to withhold text. A black box may hide something important, but its shape does not say what.

Institutional failure is itself a finding

The jail failures documented by the Inspector General do not need a murder theory to be grave. Record the failure exactly; do not make its emotional force carry an extra conclusion.
