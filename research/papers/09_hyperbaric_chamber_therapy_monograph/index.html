Structured Abstract

Complete 24-chapter, 80-page reference monograph, EPUB 3.0 e-book, 6-page clinical console wall-charts atlas, and 50-question ABMS Undersea & Hyperbaric Medicine board-review workbook synthesizing gas physics (Dalton, Henry, Boyle, Fick, Krogh cylinder), the Hyperoxic-Hypoxic Paradox (HIF-1alpha, SIRT1, PGC-1alpha, 8-fold CD34+ stem cell mobilization), ASME PVHO-1 / NFPA 99 Chapter 14 engineering, all 15 UHMS 15th Edition indications, 43 Europe PMC peer-reviewed papers (off-label uses graded as early evidence), and 26 ClinicalTrials.gov trials.

Keywords: Hyperbaric Oxygen Therapy, HBOT, Undersea and Hyperbaric Medicine, UHMS Indications, Hyperoxic-Hypoxic Paradox, ASME PVHO-1, NFPA 99 Chapter 14, US Navy Treatment Table 6
